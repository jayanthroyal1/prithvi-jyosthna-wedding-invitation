The Sacred Rituals

కళ్యాణ వైభోగమే

Each Vedic ritual is a blessing for the life they begin together.

  1. ౧

    స్నాతకం

    Snathakam

    The groom completes his student life, takes the sacred thread and is blessed to enter married life.

  2. ౨

    కాశీ యాత్ర

    Kashi Yatra

    The groom sets off for Kashi with umbrella and staff — until the bride’s brother persuades him to marry.

  3. ౩

    ఎదురుకోలు

    Edurukolu

    The two families come face to face and welcome each other with music, flowers and joy.

  4. ౪

    కన్యాదానం

    Kanyadanam

    The bride’s parents place her hand in the groom’s, praying for her lifelong happiness.

  5. ౫

    జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

    Jeelakarra Bellam

    At the muhurtham the tera is lowered and the couple place cumin and jaggery on each other’s head.

  6. ౬

    మాంగల్య ధారణ

    Mangalya Dharana

    The groom ties the mangalasutram as “Mangalyam tantunanena” is chanted.

  7. ౭

    తలంబ్రాలు

    Talambralu

    Bride and groom shower each other with turmeric rice and pearls, wishing each other abundance.

  8. ౮

    సప్తపది

    Saptapadi

    Seven steps around the sacred fire, each one a vow for the life they share.

  9. ౯

    అరుంధతి నక్షత్రం

    Arundhati Nakshatram

    The couple is shown the Arundhati star, the timeless symbol of a devoted bond.
